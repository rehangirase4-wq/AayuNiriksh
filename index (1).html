Understanding Your Body
What is PCOS / PCOD?

A hormonal condition that deserves real attention

  • 💊
    PCOS – Polycystic Ovary Syndrome A hormonal disorder causing enlarged ovaries with small cysts. Affects periods, fertility, and metabolism.
  • 🔬
    PCOD – Polycystic Ovarian Disease The ovaries release immature or partially mature eggs which form cysts. More common and often milder than PCOS.
  • ✅
    It's manageable With the right lifestyle changes, medication, and support, most women manage symptoms effectively.
Recognise the Signs

Common symptoms to watch for

Every woman's experience is different. These are the most frequently reported symptoms.

🗓️

Irregular Periods

Infrequent, irregular or prolonged menstrual cycles

⚖️

Weight Changes

Unexplained weight gain, especially around the abdomen

💇

Hair Thinning

Hair loss on the scalp or unwanted hair on face/body

🧴

Acne & Oily Skin

Hormonal acne on face, chest and back

😴

Fatigue

Constant tiredness even after adequate rest

😔

Mood Swings

Anxiety, depression, and emotional fluctuations

🤰

Fertility Issues

Difficulty conceiving due to irregular ovulation

🩸

Pelvic Pain

Pain or discomfort in the lower abdomen area
